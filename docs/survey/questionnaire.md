# SafeBalance survey: getting paid safely

**Purpose:** find out, before we build more, whether workers and clients want this, what they'd pay, and which features matter most to contractors and project managers.
**Time to complete:** about 8 minutes. Mostly tap-to-answer.
**Who it's for:** artisans, freelancers, online vendors, contractors, project managers/QS/engineers, estate managers, and people who hire them.

The Google Form version (built by `create-form.gs`) branches by role: each person sees **About you**, then **one** of sections A, B or C, then sections D–F for everyone.

Text shown at the top of the form:

> We're building a service that makes sure workers get paid their balance and clients only pay for work that's done. Your answers will shape what we build. It takes about 8 minutes. Answers are anonymous unless you choose to leave your contact at the end. We use them only for research and, if you agree, to invite you to try the service early.

## D. Our idea (everyone)

Text shown before the questions:

> **How SafeBalance would work:** Before work on each stage starts, the client pays into a locked account held by a licensed bank or payment company. The worker can see the money is there, does the work, and sends photos as proof. The client approves and the worker is paid straight away. If the client doesn't respond within 72 hours, the worker is paid automatically. If there's a problem, the money stays locked until a neutral mediator settles it. It works on WhatsApp, so no app is needed.

1. **Would you use a service like this?** *(required; scale 1–5: 1 = Definitely not, 5 = Definitely yes)*
2. If you're paid for work: would most of your clients agree to lock the money before you start? Most would · About half · Few would · I don't know · I don't get paid for work
3. If you hire: would you lock the money before work starts, knowing you get it back if the job isn't done? Yes, always · Only for new workers · Only for big jobs · No · I don't hire people
4. What would make clients more willing to lock their money? *(tick all)* Full refund if the job isn't done · A short warranty after the job · Seeing the worker's ID and reviews · Paying in parts before work starts · A small fee · Knowing which bank holds the money
5. **For a ₦100,000 job, what fee would feel fair to the person paying it?** *(required)* ₦1,000 (1%) · ₦2,000 (2%) · ₦3,000 (3%) · ₦5,000 (5%) · More than ₦5,000 · No fee would feel fair
6. Who should pay the fee? The client · The worker · Split 50/50 · It depends on the job
7. After a stage is approved, how fast should the worker get the money? Instantly · Same day · Within 2 days · It doesn't matter much
8. Would you verify your identity with BVN or NIN to receive money? Yes, either · Only NIN · Only BVN · No
